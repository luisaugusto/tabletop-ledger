Tabletop Ledger support

Help with your iPhone board game journal.

Log a game

Start a session, enter the game and players, and save it. Scores, winners, notes, ratings and photos are optional. You can choose more than one winner for a tie or a team win.

Find or edit a session

Open a past session from the journal to see the details. Tap Edit to correct the record.

Where are my sessions stored?

Tabletop Ledger stores your journal on your device. It does not require an account or sync your journal between devices. Your device's backup settings may affect whether your journal can be restored.

Get help or report a problem

Visit the public support tracker. If reporting a problem, include your app and iOS versions and the steps that led to it. Do not post journal entries, personal photos, or other private information.
