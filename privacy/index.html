Privacy policy

How Tabletop Ledger handles your information.

Your journal

Tabletop Ledger does not require an account. Your game sessions, player names, scores, notes, ratings and optional photos are stored in the app's storage on your iPhone. The app does not send this information to the developer, run analytics or advertising, or track you across apps and websites.

Photos and backups

You can choose a photo with the system photo picker or use the camera if you grant permission. The app stores a resized copy in your journal. You can change camera permission in Settings. Tabletop Ledger does not sync your journal through iCloud. Apple's device backups may include app data, depending on your settings.

Removing your data

To remove the local journal, delete the app from your device. Copies may remain in existing device backups. Manage those separately in your device settings.

Support and this website

This site is hosted by GitHub Pages. GitHub handles requests to its services under the GitHub Privacy Statement. The support tracker is public; do not post private information there. We may update this policy on this page if the app's data practices change.
